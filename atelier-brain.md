## 🎨 TOP 3 RISING CRAFT AESTHETICS

### 1. **Quiet Luxury Craft** *(High Momentum)*
Understated, material-forward pieces with minimal ornamentation. Think undyed linen, natural horn, unbleached beeswax, matte oxidized brass. Buyers are rejecting maximalism in favor of *felt* quality over visible effort. Heavily indexed among 28–45 urban professional buyers.

> **Signal:** Search volume for "minimal handmade," "natural finish," and "undyed wool" consistently trending upward on Etsy and Pinterest since Q3 2024.

---

### 2. **Dark Botanicals / Gothic Naturalism** *(Fast Rising)*
Pressed botanicals, dried florals in deep jewel tones, wax seals, and nature-death symbolism (moths, seed pods, bare branches). Bridges witchy aesthetics with editorial luxury. Strong crossover with stationery, candle, and jewelry categories.

> **Signal:** "Dark floral," "memento mori," and "dried botanical art" seeing 30–40% YoY search growth on craft platforms.

---

### 3. **Wabi-Sabi Ceramics & Textile Revival** *(Sustained + Deepening)*
Irregular forms, visible kiln marks, kintsugi-inspired repair aesthetics, and hand-stitched visible seams in textiles. Buyers are actively seeking proof of human making — imperfection *is* the premium signal. Now moving from ceramics into leather goods and paper crafts.

> **Signal:** "Handmade ceramic," "irregular pottery," and "visible stitch" remain top-converting search terms in the $65–$220 price band.
